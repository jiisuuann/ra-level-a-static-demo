SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY

A demonstration, not a market report.

All university and city names, schematic positions, dates and prices are fictional examples. A small deterministic formula creates the sample in your browser without reading any private files or calling a server API.

How to read this demonstration
  • The sample covers invented observations during 2022–2024. USD formatting is illustrative.
  • Each filter affects the map aggregates, cards, city table, comparison and quarterly trend. No-results selections display an explicit empty state.
  • Selections with fewer than 30 observations are marked Sparse. All sample prices are invented and safe to inspect.
  • No professor-provided records, private aggregates, property coordinates, addresses or identifiers are included. The diagram's x/y positions are layout units only.
  • No external map, analytics, fonts, data requests or runtime packages are used.
